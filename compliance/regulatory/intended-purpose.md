# Intended Purpose Document — Universal Diagnostic Operator (UDO 2032)

**Document ID:** UDO-REG-001  
**Version:** 2.0.0  
**Status:** DRAFT — REQUIRES FORMAL REGULATORY & CLINICAL REVIEW  

---

## 1. Regulatory Intended Purpose Statement

> **UDO (Universal Diagnostic Operator) 2032** is a clinician-facing clinical decision-support and workflow optimization software platform. It is intended to assist qualified healthcare professionals in structured patient assessment, triage risk stratification, clinical information synthesis (including GDT/ALBIS practice management system data integration), and medical documentation drafting. 

> **UDO does NOT independently diagnose, prescribe medication, or replace professional clinical judgment.** All clinical recommendations generated by UDO require verification, review, and final decision-making by an appropriately qualified physician or medical professional.

---

## 2. Target Users & Patient Population

* **Intended Users:** Qualified physicians, specialists, nurses, and medical office assistants (MFA) in primary care, neurology, psychiatry, and emergency consultation.
* **Patient Population:** Adult and pediatric patients presenting for general medical, neurological, psychiatric, or urgent triage consultation.
* **Clinical Environment:** Outpatient clinics, medical care centers (MVZ), hospital emergency departments, and telemedical consultation centers across Germany and the European Union.

---

## 3. Indications & Clinical Use Cases

1. **Deterministic Red-Flag Triage:** Instant identification (<1ms) of acute life-threatening symptoms (stroke, myocardial infarction, acute respiratory failure, anaphylaxis, status epilepticus, severe psychiatric crisis).
2. **Clinical Decision Support (CDS):** AI-assisted evidence synthesis and differential considerations for neurological and general medical cases.
3. **GDT / ALBIS Interoperability:** Automated import and export of patient health records compliant with German practice management standards.
4. **Gutachten & Document Generation:** Automated drafting of medical reports, sick notes (AU), and specialist referrals under physician oversight.

---

## 4. Contraindications & Limitations

* **Autonomous Operation:** UDO is strictly contraindicated for fully autonomous diagnostic or therapeutic execution without human physician oversight.
* **Non-Qualified Personnel:** Not intended for unassisted use by laypersons or patients without clinician supervision.
* **Uncalibrated Medical Claims:** AI-suggested confidence scores represent model heuristic agreement and must not be interpreted as absolute diagnostic probability.

---

## 5. Human Oversight & Escalation Controls

* Every AI recommendation presents clear action buttons: **[ACCEPT]**, **[MODIFY]**, **[REJECT]**, **[ESCALATE TO SENIOR PHYSICIAN]**.
* Clinician actions are recorded in an immutable, AES-256 encrypted audit trail for medicolegal compliance (BGB § 630f / GDPR Art. 9).
